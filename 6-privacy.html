Privacy Policy

Maddy - voice assistant - updated 7 Nov 2026

Thodkyat

Maddy cha server ch nahi. Tuza sagla data tujhya device var rahato. Amhi (Maddy team) kahi collect, store kiwa sell karat nahi.

Kay data kuthe rahato

  1. Reminders, timers, settings - fakt tujhya device chya storage madhe (localStorage). Amchya kade kahihi jat nahi, karun server ch nahi.
  2. Mic / voice input - tu bolte te tujhya browser chi speech recognition service text madhe convert karte. Chrome vaparlas tar ti Google chi service ahe; tya audio var Google chya privacy policy cha niyam lagto. Amhi audio record kiwa store karat nahi.
  3. Voice reply (bolne) - tujhya device chya on-device text-to-speech ne hoto. Internet nahi lagta.
  4. AI prashna-uttar - tu vicharla to prashna text mhanun free API la jato (text.pollinations.ai). Mhanun personal, bank, password kiwa secret mahiti AI prashnat taku nako. Internet nasel tar AI band, baki sagla chalu.
  5. Music - "play" mhanles ki YouTube navin tab madhe ughadto. Titha Google/YouTube chi policy lagte.

Cookies ani tracking

Cookies nahi, analytics nahi, ads nahi, account nahi. App offline chalto (service worker cache).

Third-party services

  1. Browser speech recognition (Chrome: Google) - voice input sathi.
  2. text.pollinations.ai - optional AI answers sathi (free API).
  3. YouTube - music links sathi.

Ya pratyek service chi swatachi privacy policy ahe. Tyancha data vaparavar Maddy cha control nahi.

Data kase delete karayche

Browser settings > site data clear kela ki sagla local data (reminders vagere) delete hoto. App uninstall kela tar pan toch parinam.

Badal

Policy badalli tar ya page var update hoil, varcha date bagh.
